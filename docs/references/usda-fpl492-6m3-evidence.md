# USDA FPL-RP-492 specimen 6M3: evidence scope

Source: Ronald W. Wolfe and Monica McCarthy, USDA Forest Products Laboratory, 1989, [official publication record](https://research.fs.usda.gov/treesearch/25690) and [original PDF](https://research.fs.usda.gov/download/treesearch/25690.pdf). The inspected PDF has 43 pages and SHA-256 `5c1b7a265e00fe40659e1709c00a0e8a71324089810f4d0c5c46d74ffdc47ca9`. Source not bundled.

- [Figure 1 (PDF page 5)](https://research.fs.usda.gov/download/treesearch/25690.pdf#page=5) identifies the 28-ft 6:12 Fink shape and shows visible chord splices. It does not dimension all joint centers or splices. The experiment describes nominal No. 2 Southern Pine 2×4 stock and toothed connector plates.
- [Test layout and Figure 7 (PDF pages 6–8)](https://research.fs.usda.gov/download/treesearch/25690.pdf#page=8) describe eight top-chord loading positions spaced 41.125 in. apart and show the reaction detail and deflection measurement locations. The authors used the same loading and reaction details in individual and assembly tests, but did not collect the same set of deflection points. The bearing-plate end pinning and slip layers are properties of the test fixture, not a complete truss restraint model.
- [Table 1 (PDF page 23)](https://research.fs.usda.gov/download/treesearch/25690.pdf#page=23) lists **10,800 lb/in** for specimen **6M3** in its individual column. The two neighboring values (14,100 and 14,000 lb/in) are assembly load interpretations and are not the isolated result. Table 1 describes comparison of the dead-to-design load range; the preceding text also describes the fitted individual linear interval through 1.25 design load for most specimens and says individual slopes were adjusted for measurement location. Treat the published value as a reported comparison slope, not a raw node displacement or a prescribed model calibration point.
- [Table B-6 (PDF page 43)](https://research.fs.usda.gov/download/treesearch/25690.pdf#page=43) lists this same 6M3 specimen among medium-MOE trusses. In the report's TC1–4, BC1–2 and WB1–4 column order, the measured member MOEs are respectively **1.94, 1.58, 1.46, 1.99 / 1.46, 1.74 / 1.87, 1.85, 1.81, 1.81** million psi. The printed numbers were inspected in the scanned table; PDF text extraction omitted the data rows.

## Companion report: same specimen, different test

The USDA [FPL-RP-471 companion report](https://www.fpl.fs.usda.gov/documnts/fplrp/fplrp471.pdf), by Wolfe, Percival and Moody (1986), confirms the identical 6M3 MOE row in Table A2. Its Figure 3 identifies member classes and orders their positions left to right in the test apparatus. Do not copy those test conditions to FPL-RP-492: FPL-RP-471 describes the Small Homes Council setup, tested horizontally with 28 top-chord forces at 12 in. intervals. The four-inch reaction pads were 28 ft outside-to-outside with 27 ft 8 in. between reaction centers. FPL-RP-492 describes a different eight-point arrangement. The companion narrows member identification but does not establish full centerline coordinates, actual splice and joint stiffness, or equivalence of the measured displacement channels.

## Qualification hold

Use the specimen as an independent physical reference. Do not report a Woodtruss match: exact as-built node coordinates, exact position offsets for the named stiffness pieces, truss-level restraint/compliance, per-specimen load and deflection series, and the adjustment needed to compare the published slope with a specific model output have not all been verified. It is not a 50-ft Double Fink and does not validate capacity, connections, code compliance, or fabrication.
