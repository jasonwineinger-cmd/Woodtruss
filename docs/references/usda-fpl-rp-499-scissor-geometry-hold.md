# USDA FPL-RP-499 Figure 1(c): exact scissor geometry held

**Source:** Ronald W. Wolfe and Timothy LaBissoniere, *Structural Performance of
Light-Frame Roof Assemblies II: Conventional Truss Assemblies*, USDA Forest
Service, Forest Products Laboratory, April 1991.
[Official USDA report PDF](https://www.fpl.fs.usda.gov/documnts/fplrp/fplrp499.pdf);
Figure 1(c), illustration ML90 5404, in “Individual Truss and Assembly Design.”

## Verified in the indexed official text

- Figure 1(c) is the 6/3:12 scissors configuration, distinct from the two Fink
  configurations in (a) and (b). The tested trusses were designed for a 28-ft
  span at 2 ft on center. The figure caption identifies No. 2 Dense Southern
  Pine and 20-gauge metal connector plates as descriptive test-specimen data,
  not product specifications.
- The methods say the scissors configuration was chosen to study horizontal
  restraint at the bearings. For individual scissors tests, horizontal and
  vertical forces were measured at both reactions. The full roof assembly had
  distinct restraint and load-transfer conditions.
- The prose identifies a bottom-peak web-to-chord connection measurement and
  discusses king-post pullout at the upper peak. This does not enumerate the
  complete web layout or locate its panel points.

## Hold: no exact Figure 1(c) benchmark

The indexed USDA PDF text exposed the caption and methods but not the figure
artwork or a dimensioned graph. Direct opening and PDF page-raster requests
did not return the original illustration during verification. Therefore the
specimen's ordered coordinates, member incidence, bearing and joint
idealization, and exact load locations cannot be verified from the material
read. Equal headline slopes and span do not establish equivalence to the
existing parametric scissor topology. In particular, do not substitute generic
quarter points, web connectivity, pin/roller assumptions or source design loads
as an exact reconstruction.

To lift the geometry hold, obtain the original USDA page image or figure
master ML90 5404, verify graph connectivity and each needed coordinate against
dimensioned source evidence, and separately identify the particular test's
load application and support conditions before comparing responses. If the
figure is only schematic, retain the coordinate hold rather than scale it
into an exact model. No source geometry, loads or capacities were added to
the solver; this note provides no design or fabrication approval.
