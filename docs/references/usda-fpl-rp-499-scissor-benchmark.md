# USDA FPL-RP-499: measured scissors-truss stiffness reference

Source: [USDA Forest Products Laboratory Research Paper FPL-RP-499, *Structural Performance of Light-Frame Roof Assemblies, Part II*](https://www.fpl.fs.usda.gov/documnts/fplrp/fplrp499.pdf). Page numbers below are printed report pages. Figure 1(c) and its caption (p. 2) identify a 28-ft **6:12 top / 3:12 bottom** scissors configuration, one of three configurations tested. The reported span and slopes identify the specimen family, not a validated centerline/member-incidence transcription.

## What the eight individual readings mean

Table 2 (p. 15), Scissors → Individual, gives stiffness slopes for the eight specimens placed in the assembly, **tested as individual trusses** first. In Table 2's west-end location order 1–8:

| Location | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Individual stiffness (lb/in) | 6,160 | 5,850 | 5,920 | 4,600 | 5,090 | 5,680 | 4,810 | 4,510 |

The exact arithmetic mean of the **displayed** readings is 5,327.5 lb/in; the table lists 5,328 lb/in and coefficient of variation 11%. That is not an unrounded estimate from raw laboratory data. Table 1 separately names TS1 at 6,160 lb/in and includes extra specimens outside these eight; do not substitute its broader average. Table 2's Assembly A/B rows reflect sheathing/nailing and load sharing, not repeat measurements under the same isolated boundary conditions.

The plot comparison preceding Table 2 (pp. 14–15) uses a least-squares slope of the sum of measured truss reactions versus the mean displacement of three measured node points. Preserve that response definition: the slope is not a bending modulus, peak displacement, single-node load/deflection ratio, or stiffness of the assembly. The report describes triaxial load-cell instrumentation at both bearings for individual scissors tests; the ideal pin/roller study is not automatically the same restraint. The setup, load interval, gauges and specimen identity must be matched before comparing numerical slopes.

## TS1 and reproduction hold

Appendix A Table A-2 lists material MOE for TS1 as chord and member-specific observations. Its outer South-1 web entry has a dash keyed to the missing-record footnote; it is not zero, a symmetric copy from the other side, or permission to assign the mean modulus. Retain that member property as missing. Verify the visual header-to-member map against Figure 1(c) before enrolling the other values; the indexed table header is ambiguous on repeated web labels. Pre-cut lumber MOE observations do not supply joint stiffness.

**Reproduction held.** A defensible future comparison needs the exact specimen's member/joint coordinates and gauges, bearing restraint and load application mapping, and measured property/connection conventions without tuning an unknown input to the target slope. The published result is physical test evidence for a scoped historical specimen family, not verification of Woodtruss's current topology, a serviceability threshold, a transferable material or plate property, or fabrication authority.
