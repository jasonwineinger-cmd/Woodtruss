# Connector plate resistance mechanisms and label evidence

**Source review only.** This note does not select a plate, evaluate a design, qualify a press, or release fabrication.

## The reports distinguish process and resistance mechanism

- **Alpine / ITW:** [ICC-ES ESR-1118](https://cdn-v2.icc-es.org/wp-content/uploads/report-directory/ESR-1118.pdf), §4.1 (p. 2), includes hydraulic and pneumatic presses, the specified staged roller route, and the specified combined partial-embedment and stationary finish-roller route under its hydraulic-platen data category. It separately limits its single-pass roller data to rollers of at least 18 inches in diameter. Table 1 (p. 5; [ICC-ES alternate report path](https://icc-es.org/Reports/pdf_files/ESR-1118.pdf)) distinguishes these two embedment categories for lateral tooth resistance, with plate model, lumber, orientation and gross/net area basis kept separate. Table 2 concerns steel resistance, not another substitute for the Table 1 tooth data. This describes stated conditions of the evaluated product family, not operating instructions, an equipment qualification or a condition that transfers to another manufacturer.
- **MiTek:** The [ICC-ES ESR-1988 product report](https://icc-es.org/wp-content/uploads/report-directory/ESR-1988.pdf), reissued December 2025 and revised August 2026, describes full-depth embedment and hydraulic-platen, staged roller and combined routes in §4.1. Table 1 names hydraulic-platen lateral tooth resistance per plate; Table 2 records steel tension and directional shear per pair, not single-pass roller tooth resistance. Table 3 is specific to the two listed hinge models and their paired configuration. Do not infer a MiTek single-pass category or import Alpine's criterion. Retain per-face versus per-pair basis, signed orientation, and each exact modeled mechanism as separate evidence fields.

Treat condition hierarchies as report-specific. Alpine §5.1 makes its report govern a conflict with published instructions; MiTek §5.1 instead calls for the more restrictive condition. Alpine §5.5 excludes its listed loads on treated wood; MiTek §4.2.1 says the numbers are unadjusted and refers to separately applicable treatment-manufacturer evidence. These differences do not approve any material or process; wet-service and corrosion provisions do not supply missing treatment evidence.

## A package label is a pointer, not a product evaluation

MiTek ESR-1988 §7.1 identifies the embossed model and requires the box to identify MiTek, model, and either ESR-1988 or ESR-2685. [ICC-ES ESR-2685](https://icc-es.org/wp-content/uploads/report-directory/ESR-2685.pdf) is an index: its [accessible ICC-ES publication](https://icc-es.org/reports/pdf_files/ICC-ES/ESR-2685.pdf), §§1–2, directs a reviewer to the specific product report for installation, evaluated code, resistance and use limitations. The linked report-directory PDF did not resolve in this check; the accessible ICC-ES PDF verified the index role. Keep the observed marking, package report token, index mapping, and product-specific report identity/revision separate. An index token alone does not prove the embossed model, contents or stock provenance. Do not match a similar name, gauge, or label across manufacturers.

Keep a jurisdictional attachment separate from the issuer's report even when both filenames contain the same ESR number. For example, the [Florida FL2197 R12 attachment](https://www.floridabuilding.org/upload/PR_Instl_Docs/FL2197_R12_II_ESR-1988%20Dec%202025.pdf) has a December 2025 filename but its Florida supplement prints December 2024 reissue; the issuer's August 2026 report is distinct. Do not infer byte equality, common revision scope or current jurisdictional acceptance from tokens, names, or dates alone.

## Library review boundary

Record publication identity and revision separately from exact observed product/lot marking, report-index lookup, resistance mechanism, and process evidence. A local equality or provenance-record check is not issuer authentication, inventory inspection, source license, or proof that the identified product, wood, tooling and embedment process agree. Unknown equipment configuration and validated QA stay held, including a C-press known only by type name. These public references do not import capacities, authorize equipment operation, or generate project, purchasing or release decisions.


The library-only `reviewScopedPlateReportEvidence` combines separately authored marking and resistance-basis receipts with the exact dated local product report. It rejects a mismatched source locator, method, family, section basis, or index substituted for the product report. Matching declarations are documentary equality only, not approval of an actual product or route.
