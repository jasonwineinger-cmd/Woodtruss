# TPI 1-2022 and MiTek ESR-1988: plate source intake only

## Identify the publications separately

- [TPI's ANSI/TPI 1-2022 edition and scope](https://www.tpinst.org/tpi1-update): ANSI approval December 23, 2022. The standard covers materials, member and joint design, connector testing, manufacturing quality assurance, and design responsibilities for metal-plate-connected wood trusses. The [publisher's downloads](https://www.tpinst.org/freedownloads) link the [edition-specific errata](https://www.tpinst.org/s/240826-TPI-1-2022-Errata.pdf) under August 26, 2024, and list formal TAC interpretations separately. Preserve the issuer's errata document label as shown (one correction is individually dated August 29); don't silently replace it with the latest row date. A freely available errata sheet is not the full standard or permission to redistribute it.
- [ICC-ES ESR-1988, canonical evaluation report](https://icc-es.org/wp-content/uploads/report-directory/ESR-1988.pdf): ICC-ES issuer; MiTek Inc. report holder; reissued December 2025, revised August 2026, with the cover describing an active continuous compliance program through the end of December 2027. Preserve these as distinct observations, not as an inferred expiry, live status, or acceptance in any jurisdiction. [ICC-ES ESL-1388](https://icc-es.org/wp-content/uploads/report-directory/ESL-1388.pdf) is a separate fire-tested assembly listing, reissued December 2025 with renewal due December 2026 on its cover. Its assembly scope and identifiers cannot substitute for the plate evaluation. The [manufacturer-hosted historical ESR-1988 mirror](https://www.mitek-us.com/wp-content/uploads/files/pdf/Code%20Evaluation%20Reports/esrESR-1988.pdf) carries an older cover, so it does not identify the exact newer issuer artifact.

## Keep resistance mechanisms, product and application separate

- ESR-1988 §4.1 describes full-depth seating and several manufacturing routes: hydraulic platen, staged roller, and a combined route with a stationary finish roller. Table 1 (p. 4) is expressly titled for **hydraulic-platen embedment** and records lateral tooth resistance per unit area **per plate**. Table 2 (p. 5) is **steel tension and shear resistance** per unit length **per pair**, not an alternative roller embedment table or interchangeable tooth-holding values. Description of a roller route does not supply its independently applicable tooth-resistance evidence. The report calls for identical toothed plates on opposite joint faces (§4.2.1); do not double-count a pair-level result or silently interpret per-plate area as pair-area.
- Preserve which member side and face are being discussed, the directed plate length/width axes, grain direction, and signed action in an explicit coordinate basis. Table 1's two orientation letters distinguish plate-to-load and grain-to-load relations; Table 2 distinguishes tension net-section conditions and directional shear. Do not fold shear directions to an unsigned acute angle, collapse grain and plate axes, or assume opposite-face mirroring has already been applied. These are prerequisites for a later checked method, not a capacity implementation.
- The separate MTH18 and SMH18 hinge connectors use Table 3 and §§4.1.2, 4.2.2, and 5.8–5.12: paired top-chord use in the stated final configuration, a pin in the analytical model, and restrictions on restraint, sheathing, and application. A solver's numerical end release is not proof of the physical product, and toothed plate resistance is not a hinge substitute.
- Require an independently identified manufacturer and exact model, not a generic gauge or look-alike stock. The report identifies product markings and box labeling in §7.1; no material in inventory was inspected for this note. Graded lumber identity, species, service moisture and exposure, treatment-specific evidence, and protection against corrosion remain separate (§§4.2.1, 5.5–5.6). Published values are not pre-adjusted for fire-retardant or preservative treatment. No generic untreated or species substitution resolves an unknown stock condition.

## Local audit boundary

The generic caller-supplied audit checks only explicitly recorded publication observations, including issuer and date fields. Record role (edition, errata, or evaluation report), report holder separately from issuer where applicable, and reissue, revision, and observation dates without merging them. An internally consistent record does not authenticate the source, establish current status or rights to use it, import numerical capacities, select a product, or permit a design or fabrication release. Missing application evidence remains unresolved. In particular, knowing only that a C-press exists does not establish machine type/configuration, qualification for the reported method, embedding performance, or process QA. The evaluation report establishes plate design values within its scope, not approval of the truss's design, manufacture, or installation (§5.3). This note supplies no production instructions or approval.

`reviewDatedLocalPlateReport` separately requires exact local authorization and SHA-256 bytes and an explicitly dated matching issuer observation. An express through-month-end statement may be compared through that month under the caller's same-day record, while a month-only renewal statement is held from the start of its named month rather than treated as an expiry date. Both are documentary checks, not an opinion on issuer status or a statement of applicability.
